## ALTERNATIVE SIMPLIFIED PROMPT

Create a wide landscape scientific figure showing the BRAVE closed-loop alloy design workflow. White background. Three panels arranged horizontally, connected by two clean right-pointing arrows.

**Panel 1 (left) — Design space.** Title above the panel: "8-element FCC HEA space". Show a triangular / barycentric composition diagram in soft pastel fill (light teal #E6F5F5) with a curved dark-teal (#007A7A) boundary line separating a smaller upper-right region colored light red (#F5E6E6). Label the pastel-teal region "Feasible (single-phase FCC)" in small dark text. Label the pastel-red region "Infeasible (sigma phase)". Scatter about 15 small filled circles across the diagram: teal circles inside the feasible region, maroon (#500000) circles inside the infeasible region, and 3 highlighted larger teal circles sitting exactly on the boundary line labeled "Boundary optima". A tiny "V ↑" arrow near the boundary indicating that vanadium content increases toward the infeasible side. Below the panel, small caption text "~27,000 candidates".

**Panel 2 (middle) — Risk-aware Bayesian optimization.** Title: "Risk-aware BO". A stylized closed loop with four rounded rectangles arranged in a small ring, connected by curved arrows going clockwise. Boxes in this order (top → right → bottom → left):
1. "GP surrogates" (pastel green #C8E6C9)
2. "Feasibility classifier" (pastel red #F5E6E6)
3. "Risk-weighted acquisition" (pastel orange #FFE8D1)
4. "Experiment batch" (pastel blue #D4E4F7)

At the center of the ring, a small maroon (#500000) icon of a beaker or crucible. Below the panel, small caption "3 iterations × 16 alloys".

**Panel 3 (right) — Discovered alloys.** Title: "Boundary-adjacent optima". A compact stress–strain curve on white background: x-axis "Strain", y-axis "Stress (MPa)". Two clean curves, both starting near zero and rising: a dark teal (#007A7A) curve labeled "BRAVE alloy" reaching ~1480 MPa at ~50% strain, and a lighter gray curve labeled "Baseline HEA" reaching only ~800 MPa. A small annotation "V = 24 at.%" near the peak of the BRAVE curve. Below the panel, small caption "UTS/YS > 4, ductile".

Connecting arrows between panels: a thick pastel-teal (#007A7A) rightward arrow between Panel 1 → Panel 2 labeled "sample", and another between Panel 2 → Panel 3 labeled "measure & learn".

Style: 2.5D stylized vector art with soft drop shadows. NOT photorealistic. NOT flat. ARM MIP color palette (pastel fills #E6F5F5, #F5E6E6, #C8E6C9, #D4E4F7, #FFE8D1 with dark accents #007A7A, #500000, #2E7D32, #1E5A8C, #C55A11). Clean sans-serif font (small labels only, 2-3 words each). Science/Nature graphical-abstract quality: compact, scannable, no decorative borders, no dimension numbers, no extra text outside the labels called out above. The whole composition should read left-to-right at a glance.
